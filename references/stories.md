# Stories — real anecdotes only

Empty until you add real ones. A skill generating a blog post may use **at most one** story per post, picked from this file, and must never invent one. If this file has nothing relevant to the topic, skip the story — don't fabricate a stand-in.

## Why this matters
`content/brand/brand.md` is explicit: never invent testimonials, case studies or anecdotes. A believable-sounding fake story is worse than no story, because it's a lie you're one awkward client conversation away from being caught in.

## How to add one
Short is fine — three sentences is a real story. Add:
```
### <one-line title>
**Used for topics like:** <what kind of post this fits — e.g. "slow websites", "DIY site horror stories", "redesign hesitation">
<the actual anecdote, in your own words, true, specific, with a real (or anonymized-but-real) detail>
```

## Good sources to raid for these
- A client call where someone described their old website in a way that made you laugh or wince.
- The first website you ever built, and what was wrong with it.
- A specific moment a slow page or missing click-to-call cost someone a lead (yours or a client's, confirmed, not assumed).
- Something that happened during a 24-hour Starter turnaround.

Nothing below this line yet — add the first one after your next client project or call.
