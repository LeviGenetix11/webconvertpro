# Voice

Source of truth for tone is `content/brand/brand.md`. This file expands it into rules you can check a draft against before publishing.

## Who's talking
A straight-talking web designer in Ajax who builds for contractors, trades and other local service businesses. Confident, direct, plain. Talking to a busy owner between jobs, not to a marketing department.

## Sentence rhythm
- Short sentences. Vary length, but default short.
- Lead with the benefit or the answer, then explain.
- One idea per sentence. Cut the second clause if it's just hedging.
- Active voice. "We build it first" not "It will be built first."
- Numbers over adjectives: "live within 24 hours" beats "lightning fast."

## Words we don't use
cheap, basic, template, simple, easy, unlock, leverage, seamless, world-class, game-changer, cutting-edge, "in today's fast-paced world", "dive into", "at the end of the day", "it's important to note that", "whether you're a... or a...".

Say instead: affordable or fixed-price, essential, proven layout, fast, straightforward.

## Tells that it's AI-written — delete these on sight
- Any sentence starting "In today's digital landscape/fast-paced world..."
- Throat-clearing before the point ("When it comes to websites, there are many factors to consider...")
- Triple-adjective runs ("fast, reliable, and professional")
- Exclamation marks. We don't use them.
- Emoji in body copy.
- A summary paragraph that just repeats the intro in other words.
- Vague superlatives with no number behind them ("blazing fast", "tons of leads"). If you can't cite a real number from `content/brand/offers-and-pricing.md` or a client result, cut the claim or soften it to a stated method ("built to load in under 2 seconds").

## Formatting
- Canadian English spelling (colour, optimise is American-fine for SEO terms people search, but prefer "optimize" since that's the searched term — match what people type, not the dictionary).
- Prices as `$499` (no decimals for round numbers), one-time or `/month` spelled out, CAD implied unless stated.
- Headings are statements or questions, not labels. "How is a website live in one day?" not "Speed."
- Short paragraphs: 1–3 sentences on the web.

## Proof rules (non-negotiable — see `content/brand/brand.md`)
Never invent testimonials, case studies, client names, review counts or stats. If `references/stats.md` and `references/stories.md` don't have a real number or anecdote for the point you want to make, make the point a different way (a method, a guarantee, a process step) instead of a fabricated proof point.
