# Opinions — hot takes you'll actually defend

Empty until you add real ones. A skill generating a blog post may use **at most one** opinion per post, picked from this file, backed by a number from `references/stats.md` where possible. Don't let Claude invent a "strong opinion" on the fly — a generated hot take with no conviction behind it reads exactly like the AI slop this whole workflow exists to avoid.

## How to add one
```
### <the opinion in one line>
**Used for topics like:** <what kind of post this fits>
<2-4 sentences making the actual argument, in your voice, backed by a real number if you have one>
```

## Starter prompts to write your first few from
These are prompts to answer for yourself, not opinions to copy — write your own answer, then paste it in above:
- What's the most common thing a new client's old website got wrong, that you'd bet money is also wrong on half the sites you see?
- Is there a popular piece of web design or SEO advice you think is actually bad advice for a local service business specifically?
- What's something most web design agencies oversell that you deliberately don't (e.g. "we don't do X because it doesn't move the needle for a trade business")?
- Is a flashy custom design usually worth the extra cost for a contractor vs. a fast, proven layout? What's your honest answer, not the sales answer?
