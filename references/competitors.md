# Competitors — fill in as you research

This file is empty by design until it has real research in it. Don't invent competitor names, claims or numbers.

## How to use this file
When a skill (`/blog` or `/service`) researches the top-3 ranking pages for a keyword, log what's actually true about them here — in your own words, from pages you actually read:
- Domain and the page URL
- What they cover that we should cover too
- What they're missing that we can do better (this is the opening — "stealing ground they missed")
- Roughly how long their page is, so new pages can target a comparable length
- Whether they're a real local competitor (another GTA web designer) or an unrelated big brand that happens to rank (Wix, Squarespace, Fiverr listings) — treat those two cases differently. You're not trying to out-brand Wix; you're trying to out-local-specific them.

## Format for each entry
```
### <keyword researched>
- **<domain>** (<url>) — <what they do well> / <what they skip>
```

## Known players worth checking when you research GTA web design or local SEO keywords
(Add once you've actually looked at their current pages — don't assume these notes stay accurate.)
- Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy Studio — DIY builders, usually outrank on brand, not on local intent.
- Clutch, DesignRush — directories that rank for "best web design company" style queries. Worth getting listed on, not worth trying to outrank for that exact query.
- Fiverr/Upwork freelancer listings — rank for very cheap, very broad terms. Different buyer than ours (one-time lowest-bidder vs. a business wanting a result).
