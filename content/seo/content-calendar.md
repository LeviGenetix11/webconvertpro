## Rules for every post
- 1,000 to 1,800 words. Answer the question in the first 100 words.
- Use real numbers only if you can source them. Link the source.
- At least 3 internal links (a service page, another post, the contact page).
- One clear next step at the end: the free preview.
- Never invent statistics, case studies or quotes.

Copy `templates/blog-post.md` into `blog-drafts/` to start one.
