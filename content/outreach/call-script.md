# Cold call script: the free preview

Goal of the call: **get permission to send the preview and book a 15-minute walkthrough.** Don't sell on the first call.

Before you dial: find their Google profile and note the trade, city and review count. If there's no website listed, you have your opener. Build a draft preview first if you have time (copy a demo site, add their name and logo).

## Opener
"Hi [Name], it's Levi from WebConvertPro here in Ajax. Quick question: are you still taking on new customers?"

(If yes) "Great. I was looking for [trade] in [city] and couldn't find a website for [business]. Do you have one?"

## If they have no website
"That's why I'm calling. I went ahead and built you a draft homepage with your name and services on it. It takes two minutes to look at. Can I send it over?"

## If they have a website
"I had a look at it on my phone and noticed [one specific thing: slow to load, number hard to find, no quote form]. I put together a quick draft of how it could look. Want me to send it?"

## Get the next step
"What's the best number or email to send it to? I'd also like to walk you through it for 15 minutes. Would tomorrow at [time] or [time] work?"

(Book on the call. Don't send calendar links on a cold call.)

## Objections
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I'm not interested." | "No problem. Can I ask, is it because you're getting enough work, or because you've been burned by a website before?" |
| "I get all my work from referrals." | "That's the best kind. The website just makes sure referrals can find you and trust you when they Google your name. Can I send a draft?" |
| "How much is it?" | "The draft is free. If you like it, our Starter site is $499 one-time and goes live in a day — you pay once you approve it, nothing before. Custom Growth builds start at $999, half to start and half once you approve it before launch, then $349 a month for hosting and ongoing local SEO. Revisions are unlimited on both, not a capped number of rounds." |
| "I already have a guy." | "Great. Is it getting you calls? I can send you my draft so you can compare. No obligation." |
| "Just email me." | "Sure. What's your email? And what's your biggest frustration with how customers find you right now?" |

## After the call
- Send the preview link by text or email within 1 hour
- Log the call in your lead sheet (outcome, next step, date)
- Follow up: day 1 text, day 3 call, day 7 email

## Rules
- Calls to businesses are allowed. Cold **emails and texts** must follow CASL: identify yourself and your business, include contact info and an unsubscribe option, and only message businesses whose address is public and relevant to what you offer. Check the CRTC guidance.
- Be honest that you built the draft yourself. Never claim to have clients or results you don't have.
