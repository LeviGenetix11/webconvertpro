# Local Visibility Scorecard — fulfillment template

Internal template for every `/local-seo-audit/` submission. Never publish this file itself. Same rule as the website health report: MEASURED / REVIEWED / NOT ASSESSED, never a number with nothing behind it. Local search results vary by location, device and search term — never imply a guaranteed ranking outcome.

---

## 1. Business information
- Business name / trade / service area:
- Google Business Profile link (if provided):
- Report date / reviewed by:

## 2. Google Business Profile — REVIEWED
Categories (primary + secondary), hours accuracy, services listed, photo count and recency, description quality, whether it's claimed and verified.

## 3. Website local optimization — REVIEWED
Does the site name the service + city in titles/headings? Dedicated page per city/service, or one generic page?

## 4. Review presence — REVIEWED
Approximate review count, average rating if visible, recency of most recent reviews, whether the business is responding to reviews.

## 5. Business information consistency — REVIEWED
Compare name/address/phone across the website, Google profile, and whatever else is visibly discoverable. Flag mismatches, don't guess at ones you can't see.

## 6. Directory listings — REVIEWED
General impression only here; link to a full `/directory-scan/` for a dedicated check rather than duplicating that work.

## 7. Local relevance — REVIEWED
Does the business show up for a plausible local search done manually (e.g. "[trade] [city]")? Note: results vary by device/location/account history — this is a spot-check, not a verified ranking.

## 8. Competitor visibility — REVIEWED, OBSERVATIONAL ONLY
Note what 1–2 visibly higher-ranking local competitors are doing differently (more reviews, more photos, a dedicated service page) — never fabricate competitor numbers you haven't actually seen.

## 9. Recommended improvements — prioritized action plan
List in order of expected impact, 3–6 items, each one sentence.

## 10. Next steps
Close with a link to `/google-business-profile/` for ongoing local SEO support, per `references/cta.md`.
