# Local Competitor Analysis Report — fulfillment template

Internal template for `/resources/competitor-analysis` submissions. Never publish this file itself. Compares a business against up to three competitors they name (or that we find in an obvious local search if they ask us to identify them).

**Non-negotiable: every line item here must come from something publicly viewable right now** — a visit to the competitor's live website, their public Google Business Profile, or their visible public reviews. Never fabricate or estimate a competitor's traffic, revenue, rankings or conversion rate — those aren't publicly observable, so they don't belong in this report at all.

- Competitor 1 / 2 / 3 (named by the client, or found via a plain "[trade] [city]" search if they asked us to identify them):

## 2. Website quality — observed, not scored numerically
One or two sentences per business: design quality, clarity of services, how current it looks.

## 3. Mobile experience
Quick phone check of each site: readable, tap-to-call present, fast-feeling or slow-feeling.

## 4. Business positioning
How each business describes itself and what it leads with (price, speed, trust, specialty).

## 5. Visible calls to action
What each site asks the visitor to do, and how many times.

## 6. Publicly available review information
Approximate review count and rating for each, as currently shown on Google — a snapshot, not a tracked trend.

## 7. Local business information
Service area and category as each business publicly states it.

## 8. Directory presence — where verifiable
Only list a directory presence you actually checked for each business; otherwise, omit it rather than guess.

## 9. Branding quality
Logo, colour consistency, and overall visual polish, compared side by side.

## 10. Visible service offerings
What each business explicitly lists as a service; note any gaps or overlaps.

## 11. Marketing opportunities
Where the client's business is visibly behind, and where it's visibly ahead.

## 12. Recommended competitive improvements
3–5 concrete, prioritized suggestions based only on what's documented above.

## 13. Next steps
Close with a link to the most relevant service page (often `/web-design/` or `/branding/`), per `references/cta.md`.
