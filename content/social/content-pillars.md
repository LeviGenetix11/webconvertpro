# Social content pillars

Post to **Instagram, Facebook and TikTok** (Reels / Shorts). Two goals: look established to local business owners, and give them a reason to ask for the free preview.

## Pillars
| Pillar | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Before and after** | A real old site next to a rebuilt one | "Northfield's old site vs. the new one" (use demo sites until you have clients) |
| **Quick tips** | One fix a business owner can do today | "Put your phone number at the top of your site" |
| **Behind the build** | Screen recordings of building a preview | "I built this roofer a site in 15 minutes" |
| **Local proof** | Real client wins, reviews, launches | Only once you have real ones |
| **Offer posts** | The free preview, direct | "I'll build your homepage free. You pay only if you love it." |

Mix: 2 tips, 1 before/after, 1 behind the build, 1 offer per week.

## Repurpose every blog post
One post becomes:
1. A 30 to 45 second Reel: the main tip, text on screen
2. A carousel: 5 to 7 slides summarizing the post
3. A single image quote or stat card (only with sourced numbers)
4. A Facebook post linking to the article
5. A story asking a question, with the link sticker

## Rules
- Every post ends with one call to action, usually "Get your free preview" with the link in bio.
- Use real work only. Label demo sites as examples.
- Put UTM tags on every link (see `../ads/utm-guide.md`).
- No claims you can't prove. No fake testimonials.
