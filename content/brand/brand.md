# WebConvertPro brand

## One line
Websites that work as hard as you do.

## Who we serve
Service businesses in the GTA: contractors, renovators, roofers, HVAC, landscapers, painters, cleaners. Home base: Ajax, Durham Region.

## Voice
Confident, direct, plain. Short sentences. Lead with the benefit. Use numbers when we have them. Talk to a busy owner, not a marketer.

## Words we don't use
cheap, basic, template, simple, easy

Say instead: affordable or fixed-price, essential, proven layout, fast, straightforward.

## Fonts
- Headlines: **Bricolage Grotesque** (Google Fonts), weight 700 to 800
- Body: **Instrument Sans** (Google Fonts), weight 400 to 600

## Logo
`src/assets/img/logo.svg`. Cobalt rounded square with a white "W" mark.

## Proof rules
Never invent testimonials, case studies or statistics. Use real results only. Until you have client results, lead with the free preview offer and the demo sites.
