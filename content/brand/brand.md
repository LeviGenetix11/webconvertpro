# WebConvertPro brand

## One line
Websites that work as hard as you do.

## Who we serve
Home services (home improvement) businesses in the GTA: contractors, renovators, roofers, plumbers, electricians, HVAC, landscapers, painters, cleaners. Not clinics, not retail, not professional services generally — the niche is home improvement specifically. Home base: Ajax, Durham Region.

## Voice
Confident, direct, plain. Short sentences. Lead with the benefit. Use numbers when we have them. Talk to a busy owner, not a marketer.

## Words we don't use
cheap, basic, template, simple, easy

Say instead: affordable or fixed-price, essential, proven layout, fast, straightforward.

## Fonts
- Headlines: **Bricolage Grotesque** (Google Fonts), weight 700 to 800
- Body: **Instrument Sans** (Google Fonts), weight 400 to 600

## Logo
`src/assets/img/logo.png` (light background) / `logo-light.png` (dark backgrounds, used in the footer) — the real brand mark: blue monitor, green upward growth arrow, blue/green two-tone "WebConvertPro" wordmark. `logo.svg` is an older, unused icon-only draft — don't use it as the brand reference.

## Proof rules
Never invent testimonials, case studies or statistics. Use real results only. Until you have client results, lead with the free preview offer and the demo sites.

**Exception confirmed by Levi, 2026-11-26:** two AI-generated concept images on the homepage (a Google Business Profile card showing "4.9★ (127)" and a growth-chart/"#1 ranking" visual, both for the labeled-fictional "Durham Build Co." / "Oakline Renovations") use fabricated numbers baked into the image pixels. Levi explicitly approved using them despite the rule above. Both carry an adjacent "illustrative — not real client data" caption. This is a one-off, scoped exception for these two specific images, not a general license to fabricate — still never invent numbers in body copy, schema, or new images without asking first.
