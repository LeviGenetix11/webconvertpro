# UTM links

Add these to every link you post so you can see which post or ad produced each lead in Google Analytics.

Format: `https://webconvertpro.com/contact/?utm_source=SOURCE&utm_medium=MEDIUM&utm_campaign=CAMPAIGN`

| Where | utm_source | utm_medium |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram bio | instagram | social |
| Instagram post/story | instagram | organic_social |
| Facebook post | facebook | organic_social |
| Facebook/Instagram ad | facebook | paid_social |
| TikTok bio | tiktok | social |
| Google Ads | google | cpc |
| Cold email | email | outbound |
| Cold text/DM | sms | outbound |
| Business card / QR | offline | qr |

`utm_campaign` = a short lowercase name, e.g. `free-preview`, `roofers-nov`, `blog-website-cost`.

Rules: lowercase, hyphens, no spaces. Never put UTM tags on links between pages of your own site.
Keep a list of live campaign names in `ads/ad-log.md`.
