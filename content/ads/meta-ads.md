# Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads

Goal: get GTA service business owners to request a free preview. Start small, learn, then scale.

## Offer
**"We build your new homepage free. You only pay if you love it."**

## Audience
- Location: Durham Region, Toronto, York, Peel, Halton (or the cities you're calling)
- Age: 28 to 60, both genders
- Interests / behaviours: small business owners, "Facebook Page admins", construction, home improvement, landscaping, HVAC (test one at a time)
- Placements: Advantage+ placements to start

## Budget
$15 to $25 a day for 7 days per ad set. Don't judge anything before about 1,000 impressions per ad and 7 days of data. Turn off what has spent about 2 to 3x your target cost per lead with no leads.

## Angles to test (one ad set per angle)
| Angle | Hook |
|---|---|
| Free preview | "I'll build your new homepage free. You only pay if you love it." |
| No website | "Customers are Googling you. Is there a website for them to find?" |
| Old website | "Is your website from 2014? Here's what it's costing you." |
| Speed | "Your site takes 6 seconds to load on a phone. Most visitors leave at 3." |
| Price | "Professional website, live tomorrow, $499." |

## Copy template
**Primary text:**
```
[Hook]

We build a working draft of your new homepage first, with your logo, services and city. You see it before you pay a cent.

• Live in 24 hours with our Starter site ($499)
• Custom Growth sites: $999 (50% to start, 50% before launch), then $349/month for hosting and local SEO
• Built for roofers, renovators, HVAC, landscapers and more

Tap below and tell us about your business.
```
**Headline:** Free website preview for your business
**Description:** Live in 24 hours. Pay after you approve.
**Button:** Get quote / Learn more

## Creative
- 15 to 30 second video: screen recording of a preview being built
- Static: split image, old site vs new site
- Keep files in `creative/` named `YYYYMMDD-angle-format.mp4`

## Landing page
Send traffic to `https://webconvertpro.com/contact/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=free-preview`. The form is the landing page.

## Track
Cost per lead, cost per booked call, cost per client. Log in `ads/ad-log.md` weekly.
