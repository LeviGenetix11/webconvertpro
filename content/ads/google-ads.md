# Google Ads (search)

Capture people already searching for a web designer. Start with one campaign, local only.

## Campaign
- Type: Search. Goal: Leads. Networks: Search only (turn off Display and Search Partners to start)
- Location: Durham Region + Toronto (people in or regularly in those areas)
- Budget: $20 to $30 a day
- Bidding: Maximize clicks to start, then switch to Maximize conversions after about 15 conversions

## Ad groups and keywords (exact and phrase match)
| Ad group | Keywords |
|---|---|
| Small business web design | "small business web design", "web design for small business", [website design services] |
| Contractor websites | "website for contractors", "contractor website design", "roofing website design" |
| Fast / cheap-intent | "website in a day", "fast website for small business" (do not bid on "cheap") |
| Local | "web design ajax", "web design oshawa", "web design durham region", "web designer near me" |

## Negative keywords (add on day one)
free, template, diy, jobs, course, tutorial, salary, wordpress theme, wix, squarespace, shopify, logo

## Ad copy (responsive search ad)
**Headlines (up to 15, 30 characters max):**
- Free Website Preview
- See Your Site Before You Pay
- Websites Live in 24 Hours
- Web Design for GTA Trades

**Descriptions (90 characters max):**

Track the `/thank-you/` page as the conversion in Google Ads (and in GA4 as an event).
