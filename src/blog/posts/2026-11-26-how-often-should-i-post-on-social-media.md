How often should you post on social media? Three to five times a week is the realistic target for most home services businesses, enough to stay visible without burning out trying to keep up. Posting once a month doesn't build trust. Posting daily with nothing real to show isn't worth the time either.

![Monthly social media content calendar planned on a phone](/assets/img/blog/how-often-should-i-post-on-social-media/hero.jpg)

## Three to five posts a week is the realistic target

For Instagram and Facebook, three to five posts a week keeps you visible in a customer's feed without turning content into a second job. That can be a mix of finished-job photos, a quick tip related to your trade, and a reminder of the areas you serve. You don't need a post every day to stay top of mind. You need to show up often enough that your business doesn't disappear between the time someone sees you and the time they need you.

## Consistency beats frequency every time

A page that posts ten times in its first month and goes quiet after that reads the same to a customer as one that's been abandoned. Scrolling past a business's profile before calling, "last posted two years ago" doesn't say busy, it says might not be in business anymore. A steady three posts a week, every week, builds more trust over a year than any single burst of activity.

![Scrolling a business's social media feed on a smartphone](/assets/img/blog/how-often-should-i-post-on-social-media/posting-consistency.jpg)

## What to post when you don't have time to post every day

You don't need new material every single day. You need a short list you can pull from:

- A photo from a finished job, with the city and the work done
- A before-and-after from a recent project
- A quick answer to a question customers actually ask you
- A reminder of the areas you serve, especially if you've added a new one
- A short clip from a job site, even filmed on a phone

Batch this once a month instead of scrambling for an idea every morning. Take ten photos on your next few jobs, pick the best four or five, and schedule them out over the following weeks.
