If you run a service business in the GTA, you have probably seen website quotes anywhere from a few hundred dollars to more than ten thousand. That range is real. The difference comes down to what you get, who does the work, and what you pay for every month afterwards.

This guide breaks down the common price levels so you can compare quotes properly.

![Chart comparing three pricing tiers](/assets/img/blog/how-much-does-a-business-website-cost-ontario/price-levels.jpg)

## The three common price levels

| Option | Typical cost (CAD) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| DIY builder (Wix, Squarespace) | $20 to $60 per month, plus your time | A business with more time than budget |
| Fast, done-for-you small business site | Roughly $500 to $1,500 once | A service business that needs to look professional now |
| Custom-designed site | Roughly $1,500 to $5,000 and up | A business competing for several services across several cities |

These are ranges, not rules. Always ask what is included before comparing prices.

![Web designer working on a laptop](/assets/img/blog/how-much-does-a-business-website-cost-ontario/changes-price.jpg)

## What actually changes the price

**Number of pages.** A five-page site (home, services, about, reviews, contact) costs less than a site with a separate page for every service and every city you serve.

**Custom design vs a proven layout.** A layout already tested for your trade gets you live faster and cheaper. A fully custom design takes longer because someone designs it around your brand from scratch.

**Writing.** Some quotes include the words on every page. Others expect you to supply them. Good copy is often the difference between a site that gets calls and one that does not.

**SEO setup.** Page titles, descriptions, headings and local business markup help Google understand where you work and what you do. Ask whether this is included or sold separately.

**Features.** Online booking, quote calculators and integrations all add work.

![Small business owner reviewing an invoice](/assets/img/blog/how-much-does-a-business-website-cost-ontario/costs-forget.jpg)

## The costs people forget

- **Hosting.** Some builders charge monthly. Others include it in a maintenance plan.
- **Domain name.** Usually around $20 to $30 a year.
- **Edits.** Ask whether small changes are included or billed by the hour.
- **Revisions.** Ask how many rounds of changes you get before launch.
- **Ownership.** Make sure you own your domain, content and site files once you have paid.

## What a service business should look for

A website for a contractor or trade does one job: turn a local search into a phone call or a quote request. Check that any quote you are considering includes:

1. A layout that works on a phone, since most of your customers will find you there.
2. Click-to-call and a short contact form on every page.
3. Writing that names your services and the cities you serve.
4. Basic SEO setup so Google can place you on a map and in local searches.
5. A clear price in writing, with revisions and hosting spelled out.
