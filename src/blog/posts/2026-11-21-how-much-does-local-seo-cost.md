How much does local SEO cost? Across the market, local SEO typically runs anywhere from $100 to $3,000 or more a month, with most small businesses in low-competition markets landing between $500 and $1,000, and growing businesses in more competitive trades often paying $1,000 to $2,500. That range is wide because "local SEO" can mean a basic Google Business Profile tune-up or a full monthly program of listings, reviews and ongoing optimization. What you're actually buying at each price point matters more than the number itself.

![Calculator sitting next to a local map pin icon](/assets/img/blog/how-much-does-local-seo-cost/calculator-map-pin.jpg)

## Why the range is so wide

A $500-a-month package and a $3,000-a-month package can both be called "local SEO" and have almost nothing in common. The price depends on how competitive your trade and city are, how much of the work is done for you versus just advised, and whether it's a one-person freelancer or an agency with a full monthly process. Compare quotes by what's delivered each month, not by the headline number.

## What typically drives the price

**Competition in your trade and city.** A roofer in a city with fifteen established competitors costs more to rank than a niche service with three.

**How much is actually done each month.** Weekly profile posts, active review requests, and ongoing citation management cost more to deliver than a one-time profile setup with occasional check-ins.

**Whether your website supports it.** Local SEO sends people somewhere. If the site it sends them to is slow or has no basic on-page SEO, part of what you're paying for is wasted on a destination that can't convert the visit.

**Reporting and communication.** A monthly report that actually tells you what changed costs more to produce than a dashboard you're left to interpret alone.

![Business owner reviewing a monthly report on a laptop](/assets/img/blog/how-much-does-local-seo-cost/monthly-report-review.jpg)

## What's usually included at each level

| Price range (CAD/month) | What you typically get |
|---|---|
| $100 to $500 | Basic profile setup or occasional check-ins, little ongoing work |
| $500 to $1,500 | Regular posting, review requests, citation management, monthly reporting |
| $1,500 and up | The above plus more aggressive content, link building or multi-location management |

These are general market ranges, not our pricing, use them to sanity-check a quote you've already been given.
